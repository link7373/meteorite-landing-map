Tunnunik (formerly Prince Albert)

Tunnunik (formerly Prince Albert) is a confirmed impact crater in Canada, roughly 25.0 km across and dated to about 130-450 million years ago. It marks where a meteorite struck Earth with enough energy to leave a lasting scar on the landscape.

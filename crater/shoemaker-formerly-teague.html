Shoemaker (formerly Teague)

Shoemaker (formerly Teague) is a confirmed impact crater in Australia, roughly 30.0 km across and dated to about 1630 ± 5 million years ago. It marks where a meteorite struck Earth with enough energy to leave a lasting scar on the landscape.

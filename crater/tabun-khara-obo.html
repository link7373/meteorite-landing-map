Tabun-Khara-Obo

Tabun-Khara-Obo is a confirmed impact crater in Mongolia, roughly 1.3 km across and dated to about 150 ± 20 million years ago. It marks where a meteorite struck Earth with enough energy to leave a lasting scar on the landscape.

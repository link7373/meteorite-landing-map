Chesapeake Bay

Chesapeake Bay is a confirmed impact crater in the United States, roughly 40.0 km across and dated to about 35.5 ± 0.3 million years ago. It marks where a meteorite struck Earth with enough energy to leave a lasting scar on the landscape.

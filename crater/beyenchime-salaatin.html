Beyenchime-Salaatin

Beyenchime-Salaatin is a confirmed impact crater in Russia, roughly 8.0 km across and dated to about 40 ± 20 million years ago. It marks where a meteorite struck Earth with enough energy to leave a lasting scar on the landscape.

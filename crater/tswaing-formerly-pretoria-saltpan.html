Tswaing (formerly Pretoria Saltpan)

Tswaing (formerly Pretoria Saltpan) is a confirmed impact crater in South Africa, roughly 1.13 km across and dated to about 0.220 ± 0.052 million years ago. It marks where a meteorite struck Earth with enough energy to leave a lasting scar on the landscape.

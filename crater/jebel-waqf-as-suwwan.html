Jebel Waqf as Suwwan

Jebel Waqf as Suwwan is a confirmed impact crater in Jordan, roughly 5.5 km across and dated to about 56 - 37 million years ago. It marks where a meteorite struck Earth with enough energy to leave a lasting scar on the landscape.

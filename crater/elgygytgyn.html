Crater

El'gygytgyn

El'gygytgyn is a confirmed impact crater in Russia, roughly 18.0 km across and dated to about 3.5 ± 0.5 million years ago. It marks where a meteorite struck Earth with enough energy to leave a lasting scar on the landscape.

Zhamanshin

Zhamanshin is a confirmed impact crater in Kazakhstan, roughly 14.0 km across and dated to about 0.9 ± 0.1 million years ago. It marks where a meteorite struck Earth with enough energy to leave a lasting scar on the landscape.

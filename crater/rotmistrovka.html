Crater

Rotmistrovka

Rotmistrovka is a confirmed impact crater in Ukraine, roughly 2.7 km across and dated to about 120 ± 10 million years ago. It marks where a meteorite struck Earth with enough energy to leave a lasting scar on the landscape.
